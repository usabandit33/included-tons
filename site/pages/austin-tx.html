Austin roll-off checklist

For homeowners and small contractors in Austin, Round Rock, Cedar Park, Pflugerville, Kyle, and Buda.

Not a city form. Confirm street rules in Austin Build + Connect before a can sits in the parking lane.

Permit

Private driveway: usually no City of Austin permit. Street, sidewalk, or alley: Temporary Use of Right-of-Way (TURP) through Austin Transportation / Austin Build + Connect. Published cost is about $45 plus a tech surcharge, a few business days, longer in historic districts. Ask who files it and whether that fee is in the quote.

Price band (not your invoice)

Advertised Austin 20-yard rentals often land in the mid-$400s to mid-$500s, with published samples roughly $385–$575 before overage and extra days. Roofing and dirt will blow a 1-ton cap while the can still looks half empty.

Ask before you pay

  1. Your truck and yard, or a broker?
  2. Included tons, in writing.
  3. Overage per ton, extra-day fee, dry-run if the driveway is blocked.
  4. Who pulls the TURP if this has to sit on the street?
  5. Your merchant account, or Zelle / a $1 hold?
