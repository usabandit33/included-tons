How the extra charge happens

Every rental includes a weight cap. The landfill subtracts the empty can (tare) and bills the debris. Go over the included tons and you pay per extra ton — often $50–$100+, sometimes dressed up as cents per pound. Fifteen cents a pound is $300 a ton.

Scam patterns

  • Marketplace listing, cash or Zelle, then a real company delivers a box booked on a stolen card.
  • National site takes a card “hold,” never drops a can, bills a cancellation fee.
  • Headline price with a 1-ton cap on a roofing job.
