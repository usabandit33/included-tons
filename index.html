Austin roll-off checklist

  1. Where will it sit? Driveway or private pad: usually no City of Austin permit. Street, sidewalk, or alley: you need a TURP permit through Austin Build + Connect (often about $45 plus a small fee, a few business days).
  2. What size? 10-yard for a garage or small bath. 20-yard for a kitchen, flooring, or room cleanout. 30-yard for a bigger remodel or roof.
  3. How many tons are included? Say this out loud before you give a card. Roofing and dirt are heavy. A half-full can can still go over a 1-ton cap.
  4. What happens if you go over? Ask overage per ton and the extra-day price. “Flat rate” still gets a scale ticket.
  5. Who is bringing the truck? A local yard, or a broker who books someone else? Skip Zelle, Cash App, or a $1 hold from a name you cannot Google.
  6. Call and book. Use a number on the Austin page. This site does not take payment and does not charge you.
