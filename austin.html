Ask before you pay

  1. Your truck, or a broker?
  2. How many tons are included?
  3. Overage per ton and extra-day fee?
  4. Street placement — who files the TURP?
  5. Card on your merchant account, not Zelle?

Permit. Driveway: usually no city permit. Street or sidewalk: TURP via Austin Build + Connect, often about $45 plus surcharge, a few business days.

Advertised Austin 20-yard prices often sit mid-$400s to mid-$500s before overage. Dirt and shingles blow a 1-ton cap while the can still looks half empty.
