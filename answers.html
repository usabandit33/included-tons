How many tons are included in a dumpster rental?

Whatever the yard wrote on the quote — often 1–2 tons on a 10-yard and 2–4 tons on a 20-yard. The number is a weight cap, not how full the can looks. Ask for it in tons before you pay.

What is a dumpster overage fee?

The extra charge after the landfill weighs the load. If you go over the included tons, you pay per extra ton. Sometimes it is written as cents per pound. Fifteen cents a pound is $300 a ton.

Do I need a permit for a dumpster on my driveway?

Usually no, if the can stays on private property. A public street, sidewalk, or alley often needs a city right-of-way permit. In Austin that is a TURP through Austin Build + Connect. Other cities have their own form.

What size dumpster do I need?

10-yard: garage or small bath. 20-yard: kitchen, flooring, one-room cleanout. 30-yard: bigger remodel or roof. Size is volume. Weight is the trap — shingles and dirt fill a small cap fast.

Why was my dumpster rental bill higher than the quote?

Common reasons: overage on the scale, extra days, a dry-run because the driveway was blocked, or fees that were never in the headline price (fuel, environmental, franchise). A quote that will not name included tons is not a quote.

Is a cheap online dumpster listing safe?

Treat Marketplace + Zelle + a price far under everyone else as a warning. A real yard takes a card on a name you can find and will say included tons on the phone.
